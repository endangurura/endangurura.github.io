All code conditions target 20 variants. The Mistral denominator is 100 trial reports, not 100 distinct cases. Qwen combines valid original answers with recovered answers for previously missing cases. These are not directly comparable first-attempt completion rates.

Benchmark-accepted fixes

Qwen achieved weighted top-1 fix credit of 33.3% with restricted code access and 11.1% with full experiments available. Accepted cases covered sandbagging detection, subliminal learning, and multi-turn jailbreaking. Full credit follows the benchmark's criterion: address the sabotage and correctly explain its mechanism.

These successes demonstrate useful local-model alignment-auditing behavior. They motivate testing whether targeted fine-tuning and stronger elicitation can make accepted fixes more consistent and economical; those interventions and cost ratios were not measured here.

Interpretation and data

Missing reports are not imputed as 50%. Conditional scores on small observed subsets do not establish a model ranking or an effect of experiment access.

The paper-only and initial code-access aggregates were recomputed from published corrected entry tables. The final Mistral and Qwen follow-ups use the experiment summaries and were not separately reaggregated from per-trial tables.
